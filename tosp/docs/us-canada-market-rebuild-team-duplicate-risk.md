## Summary

One potential cross-programme numeric-conflict topic was reviewed (Late
Shipment Rate). This programme's own two source documents already disagree
with each other on the figure (see exclusions register item 4); following a
2026-07-29 source-accuracy reconciliation, this programme now states **no**
Late Shipment Rate figure anywhere in learner-facing content, so it cannot
present a number that contradicts the Amazon Team programme's own stated
figure. The Amazon Team programme is not modified by this work. No
full-curriculum duplication was found anywhere — every overlapping topic is
either cross-referenced to its owning programme or limited to the narrow,
BGCT-specific slice of that topic the source documents actually contain.
